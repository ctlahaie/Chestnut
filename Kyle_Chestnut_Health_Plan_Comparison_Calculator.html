Methodology & notes
• Kyle's plans (Gallagher, BCBSIL): 2027 Employee & Family bi-weekly base rates × 26 pays for the selected earnings band, less the Wellbeing Credit if selected. Tobacco surcharge not included. Gallagher HSA contribution: $1,000 family on PPO Essential HSA and PPO Moderate HSA.
• Spouse's plans (PAK A, C, D): per-paycheck family cost × 26 as shown on her enrollment screen. Coinsurance for PAK A and PAK C was not shown and is assumed to be 0% after deductible; PAK D is 10%. Rx cost-sharing was not shown and is assumed to be $10 / $40 / $80 after the deductible. No employer HSA funding is assumed for her plans. Please confirm with her plan summary.
• Medical costs assume one $150 office visit per $500 of medical cost (up to 12 visits). On PPO Premier, office visits are a $25 copay not subject to the deductible; all other medical costs go to the deductible, then coinsurance. On all HSA plans, all medical costs apply to the deductible first.
• Rx drug costs assumed: generic $30, preferred brand $300, non-preferred brand $500 per fill. On HSA plans, the full drug cost applies to the deductible until met. After the deductible, Gallagher HSA plans pay 20% with $7 / $35 / $60 minimums; PPO Premier is $10 / $40 / $75 with no deductible.
• Family deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums are applied to total household usage. Rx counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum. Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included.
